## The one thing only you have

**Zooza 2016 – 2020.** The app repository begins on 21 November 2020. For those years
there is no record but pictures, and no tool can recover them.

One or two a year is plenty — the home screen or the booking list shows the era. Put them
in `help_ingest/screenshots/history/` and name them with the year
(`2016-dashboard.png`); approximate is fine, the captions will say so.

Most valuable, in order:

1. **The first version anyone used in anger**, 2016 or 2017.
2. **The oldest booking form** — what your customers' customers actually saw.
3. **Anything from a rebrand**, where the colours or the logo changed.
4. **A screen that does not exist any more.** The most interesting, and the first to be
   lost.

And the part no repository holds: a sentence per era on **why** things changed. Git says
when invoicing shipped. It does not say who asked for it, or what people were doing
before it existed.
