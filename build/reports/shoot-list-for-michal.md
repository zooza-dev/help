Generated 2026-11-07. **40 sittings, 152 pictures.**

**Why these and not the rest.** The capture tool drives a browser to an address and photographs what loads. Every shot below is of a panel that only exists *after* somebody clicks something, so no address reaches it. Everything else I am doing myself — including 158 pictures the index first put in this list, which turned out to be ordinary screens I can route to.

**Each row is one sitting.** Open the screen in *Where*, click what *Open* says, photograph, next row. Ranked by how stale the articles behind them are, so top-down is the right order.

**Before you start:** English interface, light theme, browser about 1400 px wide, shoot the region not the whole desktop. Anything with money on it: use booking **#1954 Chloe Tanase (Fred Tanase)** — its payment plan is mid-flight, so the figures on screen mean something. Drop them in `help_ingest/screenshots/dialogs/`, any filename that says what it is.

**Some rows name the panel clumsily** — the index reads the heading it finds, and a few of those are a person's name or a long sentence. If a row does not make sense on the screen in front of you, skip it and tell me; the article column says what it is for.

## And the one that needs no app at all

**Zooza 2016 – 2020.** The app repository begins on 21 November 2020, so for those years nothing but pictures exists. One or two a year is plenty — the home screen or the booking list shows the era. Name them with the year (`2016-dashboard.png`), put them in `help_ingest/screenshots/history/`; approximate is fine, the captions will say so.

Most valuable: the first version anyone used in anger, the oldest booking form, anything from a rebrand, and **a screen that does not exist any more** — those are the first to be lost and the most interesting to keep.
