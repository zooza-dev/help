FLAGSHIP · GUIDED, END-TO-END
Create programmes, classes & schedules

The headline workflow. Start a brand-new programme from scratch, or add classes and schedules to one you already run. Claude interviews you, builds a full preview, and only writes once you confirm. It resolves the course, venue and room, trainer, capacity, prices, billing period and payment templates — then expands recurrence and one-off dates into exact sessions, automatically skipping holidays.

“Create an 'Autumn Swim School' programme, then set up a Tuesday class for ages 5–8 with Jane.”

EDIT & MANAGE
Manage what you've already set up

Change what you've already created — just say what needs to change. For a class: its instructor, venue or room, session length, price, registration fee, name, capacity or billing period; turn online registration on or off; or set it active, inactive or archived. When you move an instructor, venue or length, you choose how it flows to sessions that already exist — the class from now on, all upcoming sessions, or every one. For specific sessions: reschedule one, shift a whole run forward a week, or line a set up to the same time; change their instructor, room, block or length; and optionally email the enrolled clients. It looks up a trainer's named pay rate for you, works on one class or several at once, and guards big moves like shifting a class to another programme. You can also change a programme's own settings — its pricing, trial and make-up rules, and what its booking form asks. Every edit shows a preview first — exactly what changes and how many sessions are affected — and applies only when you confirm.

“Reassign Monday ballet to Bob for all upcoming sessions.”

HONEST ABOUT THE EDGES
What it can't do yet

These aren't oversights — they're on the roadmap, on purpose. We'd rather ship a smaller thing that's safe.

No taking or moving money — payments, invoicing and refunds stay in the app (setting up payment plans and instalment templates is fine).
No enrolment changes — you can capture leads and look up bookings, but not enrol, remove or transfer clients.
No cancelling sessions or days yet — you can reschedule and edit them, just not cancel.
Richer per-user sign-in is on the roadmap.
QUESTIONS, ANSWERED
New to this? Start here

If you've never connected an AI assistant to a tool before, these cover the essentials.

What exactly am I connecting? (What's an MCP server?)
Zooza AI connects through an open standard called MCP (Model Context Protocol) — a safe way for an AI assistant to talk to an outside tool, here your Zooza account. Once connected, the assistant can look things up and make changes in Zooza on your behalf, in plain language. There's nothing to install on your computer; you connect it once inside your assistant.
Can it do everything the Zooza app can?
No — and it's not meant to. Zooza AI isn't a copy of the Zooza app; it's a natural-language fast lane for the things you'd say out loud — looking things up, taking attendance, creating and editing classes, messaging clients. The web app stays the full home for everything else: payments, detailed setup, reporting and bulk work. Cramming every Zooza feature into the connector wouldn't help — an assistant works best with a focused, well-defined set of tools, and many jobs are simply faster, clearer and safer in a purpose-built screen. Think of it as talking to Zooza for the common stuff, not a second version of the app.
Which AI model should I choose — and does it matter?
Yes, it makes a real difference. Multi-step jobs like creating a class or reading a register work best on a capable, recent model (for example Claude's Sonnet-class or GPT-4-class models). Smaller or older models are faster and cheaper, but can miss steps, misread your custom terminology, or stop before finishing a guided flow. If something behaves oddly, switching to a stronger model is the first thing to try.
Do I need to be technical to use it?
No. After a one-time setup — a few clicks in your assistant's settings — you simply chat. If a step feels unclear, the conversation starters above walk you through everything.
Is my data safe?
You sign in with your usual Zooza account, so the assistant only ever sees what your role already allows. Nothing is written — including sending a message — without a preview and your explicit confirmation. It still can't take payments or change enrolments.
What does "public beta" mean? Is it free?
It means the connector is live and usable, but still growing — some features aren't built yet (see "What it can't do yet"). It's free to use during the beta, and your feedback directly shapes what we build next.
Which languages does it understand?
Slovak, Czech, German, Hungarian, Romanian and English. You can also teach it your own words for programmes, classes and sessions, and it'll use them in every future conversation.
Can it accidentally break or delete something?
Changes are previewed before they happen, and it only offers actions your role permits. Bulk deletions and risky one-shot operations aren't available. When in doubt, ask it to show you a preview first.
