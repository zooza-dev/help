HOW WHATSAPP WORKS
Two ways to message — know the difference

WhatsApp isn't open SMS. You can't message just anyone, any time. There are two modes — and whether a message costs money depends on the type and timing.

TEMPLATES
Template messages

Pre-approved by Meta, these can be sent to any client at any time. Marketing and authentication templates cost money per delivered message. Utility templates (reminders, payment notices) sent within an open customer service window are free.

FREE
Conversations

When a client messages you first, you can reply freely for 24 hours — no template needed and no charge. The 24-hour window resets every time they message again; once it closes, you can only send templates.

Clients can reply Replies aren't one-way. Clients can answer with text, voice messages and images, and you handle every conversation right inside Zooza.

Every message is sent from your own verified WhatsApp Business account — your brand and name — never from Zooza.

WHAT IT COSTS
You pay Meta directly — not Zooza

WhatsApp messaging is billed by Meta, separately from your Zooza subscription.

To send messages, you add a payment card in Meta / WhatsApp Business Manager.
All messaging charges go directly to Meta. They are not taken from your Zooza subscription.
Marketing and authentication templates cost money per delivered message (per-message pricing, as of July 2025). Utility templates sent within an open customer service window are free.
You're always in control — switch WhatsApp off at any time.

QUESTIONS, ANSWERED
Good to know

The things activity brands ask before switching on a new channel.

Who sends these messages?
You do — from your own verified WhatsApp Business account, under your own brand and name. Zooza is not the sender. (Our demo screenshots show "Zooza" only because they come from Zooza's own test account.)
Do my clients need to install anything?
No. They already have WhatsApp. They simply receive your messages and tap the links or buttons — nothing to install or sign up for.
What does it cost, and who do I pay?
You pay Meta directly. Add a payment card in Meta / WhatsApp Business Manager; all messaging charges go to Meta and are not part of your Zooza subscription. As of July 2025, Meta charges per delivered message (not per conversation). Marketing and authentication templates cost money; utility templates (reminders, payment notices) sent within an open customer service window are free; replies within a client-started conversation are always free.
Can I message anyone, any time?
Not freely. To reach a client outside an open conversation you send a pre-approved template message (which costs money). Otherwise you can only reply within the 24-hour window after a client messages you.
Can clients reply to me?
Yes. Clients can reply with text, voice messages and images, and you handle every conversation inside Zooza.
What is the 24-hour window?
When a client messages you, a 24-hour window opens in which you can reply freely, with no template and no charge. Each new message from the client resets it. Once it closes, you can only send template messages until they write again.
Do I need a separate phone number?
Yes — a dedicated number that isn't currently used in the standard WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business mobile app. Many activity brands keep one number for automation and another for manual chats.
Can I keep using the WhatsApp mobile app on that number?
No. Once a number is connected via the API it can no longer be used in the WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business mobile app. That's why a dedicated number is required.
Can I create my own templates?
Yes — there are custom template slots for your own scenarios like venue changes or cancellations. Each custom template is reviewed by Meta (typically 1–2 business days). Bold, italics and line breaks are supported; HTML and rich layouts are not.
How do I raise my message limits?
Complete Business Verification in Meta Business Manager to unlock higher messaging tiers and more templates.
Is my clients' data safe?
Messages are sent through Meta's official WhatsApp Business platform from your own verified account. You only message the clients already in your Zooza account, and you can switch the channel off at any time.
