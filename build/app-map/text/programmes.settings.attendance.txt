Attendance

If you don't require or don't want to track attendance for this programme, you can turn it off here.

Track attendance
Yes
By turning off the attendance tracking you will disable the possibility for your clients to use make-up sessions and indicate whether they'll attend the session or not. This will also cancel sending notifications about the upcoming session. This setting is useful for summer camps or other intensive daily programmes where the attendance is pretty much guaranteed.

ALLOW SESSION RESCHEDULE
Allow rescheduling of sessions
No
If you enable this, your clients can reschedule sessions themselves. The setting respects lecturers’ working hours — clients won’t be able to choose times when the lecturer is unavailable.
