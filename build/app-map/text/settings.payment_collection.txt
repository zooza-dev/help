Payment reconciliation rules

Control how strictly Zooza reconciles incoming payments to bookings.

Only reconcile payments to outstanding bookings
Yes
When on, an incoming payment is reconciled to a booking only if the booking is outstanding (awaiting payment or unpaid) AND the payment amount does not exceed what's owed. Anything else is left for manual review. When off, Zooza reconciles more aggressively — overpayments switch the booking to 'Overpaid' automatically.
