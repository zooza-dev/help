# Ask your clients what they thought

Zooza can email a programme's clients a short set of questions and collect the answers
against the programme. It is separate from Google reviews, which are public and go to
your Business Profile — this one is yours, and stays inside Zooza.

> **Navigation:** the programme's **Settings** tab → the **Feedback and reviews** tile →
> **Feedback**. Or search for *feedback*.

---

## When to ask

Two moments, and you can use either, both, or neither.

| Setting | When it sends |
|---|---|
| `Request a provisional feedback (after the 4th session)` | After the client's fourth session |
| `Request feedback after programme has finished` | After the last session of the programme |

**The mid-term one is the useful one, and it is the one people switch off.** A term's
worth of problems is still fixable after four sessions; after the last one it is a
post-mortem. If you only turn on one, turn on that.

The end-of-programme request has a different job: it is the one to read before you decide
whether to run the same thing again.

---

## What you ask

**Question list** holds the questions. Keep it short — this arrives by email to a parent
who is doing three other things, and a long form is answered by nobody or by only the
people with a complaint, which is worse than no data.

---

## Who gets asked

**Request feedback from bookings that were created after this date** is a cut-off.
Bookings made before it are never asked.

Set it on the day you switch feedback on. Without it, every client who has ever booked
this programme becomes eligible, and a good idea on a Tuesday morning turns into a mailshot
to people who finished with you two years ago.

> **It filters on when the booking was made, not on when the sessions happened.** A client
> who booked in August for a programme starting in January is judged by the August date.

---

## Reading the answers

Answers collect under **Feedback** in the left menu, where **See all programme ratings**
lists everything and each entry opens to the client's own answers.

> **Feedback is not NPS, and neither is a Google review.** Zooza has three separate things
> that all sound like "asking the client what they think": this one, the **NPS** score, and
> the public **Google review** request. A client who has given NPS feedback is deliberately
> not asked for a Google review — see [Google reviews](../faq/google-reviews-faq.md).
