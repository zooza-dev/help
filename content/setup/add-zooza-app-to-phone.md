# Using Zooza on mobile

Zooza runs as a web app — there is no separate iOS or Android app to download. Open [zooza.app](http://zooza.app) in your phone’s browser and use it directly, or add it to your home screen for quicker access.

> **Parents can do the same with their profile.** The steps below work for any page, so a parent can put their own profile on their home screen — they just start from your profile page address instead of `zooza.app`. See [Sending parents to their profile](#parents-adding-their-profile-to-the-home-screen) below.

## Add to home screen

1. Open [zooza.app](http://zooza.app) in the browser on your phone.
2. Open the share/options menu:
   - **Android** — tap the 3-dot menu (top right).
   - **iPhone** — tap the square icon with an arrow (bottom centre).
   ![iPhone — tap the square icon with an arrow (bottom centre)](../../assets/images/add-zooza-app-to-phone-02.png)
3. Select **Add to Home Screen**.
   ![Select Add to Home Screen](../../assets/images/add-zooza-app-to-phone-03.png)
4. Enter the name for the shortcut and tap **Add**.
   ![Enter the name for the shortcut and tap Add](../../assets/images/add-zooza-app-to-phone-04.png)

> **Tip for instructors:** If you use Zooza mainly for taking attendance, open the **Calendar** section first and then add that page to your home screen. The next time you tap the icon, the calendar opens directly — no extra navigation needed.

## Parents: adding their profile to the home screen

Parents often ask for "an app". Before explaining how to add one, it is worth saying why most of them never need it.

**Zooza is built around notifications, not around a parent opening it.** Almost every scenario reaches the parent by email at the moment it matters — the booking confirmation, each payment request and reminder, session reminders, changes to a session, and anything you send from Communication. Those emails carry links that open the right page directly. A parent who reads their email is never required to go looking for their profile.

So the honest answer to "is there an app?" is usually: you do not need one, everything comes to you. Parents who ask are often really asking whether they are missing something — and they are not.

**If they still want quick access**, send them their profile address — the page on your website where the profile widget lives — and the three steps above. What they get is a **bookmark** that opens full-screen with your icon. Close enough to an app for most people, and it puts their bookings and payments one tap away.

## Zooza does not send push notifications

There are none, and none are planned. Adding a page to the home screen creates a **bookmark** — a shortcut that opens full-screen with an icon. It does not install anything and it does not send anything. Nothing appears on the lock screen.

What Zooza does send:

| Channel | Used for |
|---|---|
| **Email** | Booking confirmations, payment requests and reminders, session reminders, anything you send from Communication |
| **SMS** | Messages you send from Communication |
| **WhatsApp** | Conversations and templates, if you have the integration — see [WhatsApp integration](whatsapp-integration.md) |

If a parent wants to be told about sessions and payments on their phone, WhatsApp or SMS is the answer, not a push notification.

## Log out on mobile

1. Tap your **avatar or initials** in the top-right corner of the screen.
2. Tap **Account**.
3. Tap **Log out**.

## Switch between company accounts

If you have access to more than one company (e.g. you manage multiple Zooza accounts), you can switch without logging out:

1. Tap your **avatar or initials** in the top-right corner.
2. Tap the company name or the **Switch account** option.
3. Select the company you want to work in.

> If you don't see the option to switch, your user account is linked to only one company. Contact the owner of the other account to request access.
