# A client wants to move to a later date

"Can you move us to a later date?" is four different requests wearing the same words. Sorting out which one you have takes ten seconds and saves undoing the wrong action afterwards.

## Which situation is it?

| What the client actually means | What to do |
|---|---|
| They booked a **trial** and cannot make it | Cancel that trial session and book them into another one |
| They are **enrolled** and will miss one session | They cancel the session; if make-ups are on, they book a replacement themselves |
| They want a **different day or time** from now on | **Transfer** the booking to the other class |
| They want to **start later** — next block or next term | Adjust the enrolment start, or transfer to the later class |

> **Do not reach for "Transfer to network".** That moves the registration to a *different company* in your network — another centre entirely. It is next to Transfer in the menu and it is not what "a later time" means. See [Transfer and copy](transfer-and-copy-faq.md).

## They are in a trial

**Cancel the trial session and book a new one.** For a trial this is always the right first move — do not transfer, and do not treat it as a make-up.

1. Open the trial booking → **Attendance**.
2. **Hide** the original session, so it drops out of reports and the client's view.
3. **Book a session** and pick the new date.

The booking stays as *Trial started* and the client is notified of the new date. The trial automation still runs from the new session, which is the point: transferring or cancelling instead usually breaks the follow-up sequence that turns a trial into an enrolment.

## They are enrolled and will miss one session

This is not a move at all — the enrolment stays where it is.

The client cancels that one session from their profile. If your programme has make-up sessions enabled, the cancellation generates a credit and they book a replacement themselves, at a time that suits them.

Two things decide whether that works:

- The absence must be recorded as **Cancelled**, not *Did not attend* — only Cancelled creates a credit.
- A class only becomes available for make-ups **4 days before it starts**, so a replacement in a class starting next month will not appear yet.

If nothing is being offered and you cannot see why, run the **MATKO** diagnostic rather than guessing — it names the condition that is blocking the session. See [Make-up sessions FAQ](make-up-sessions-faq.md).

If you do not use make-ups, this becomes a billing question instead: either let it go, or adjust the payment on the booking.

## They want a different day or time from now on

Now it is a real move. Use **Transfer** on the booking, and pick the class that runs at the time they want.

Open the booking → the **Class** card → **Transfer** → choose the target class.

Check two things before you submit:

- **Payments.** Tick *Do not change payments* unless you intend to reprice. Leaving it unticked replaces the payment schedule with the target class's settings.
- **The start date of the new schedule**, if you do change payments. Set it to the transfer date, not the start of the group — otherwise Zooza back-fills instalments the client has already paid.

See [Transfer and copy bookings](../guides/transfer-and-copy-bookings.md) for the full walkthrough.

## They want to start later

If they have not started at all and want the next block or term, transferring to the later class is usually cleanest — the booking, its history and its payments move together.

If they are staying in the same class but want their enrolment to begin later, change the enrolment start on the booking instead. That shifts which sessions they are counted in without moving them anywhere.

## Why there is no single "move to a later date" button

Because the four situations above need four different things to happen to attendance, credits and money — and only you know which one the client means. A single button would have to guess, and guessing wrong on payments is expensive to unwind.

When you are unsure, ask the client one question: *are you missing this one session, or do you want a different day from now on?* The answer picks the row in the table above.
