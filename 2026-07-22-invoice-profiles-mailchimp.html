►︎  What changed and how to check it (help)

Even if you never issue an invoice from Zooza, you have an invoice profile — it holds your company details and the bank account your clients pay to. What changed is that the bank account is now a record of its own, belonging to that profile, and one profile can hold several accounts.

Your existing account came across as it was. That one structural change is what unlocks everything below.

✓  IBAN, SWIFT and account holder name in one place, per bank account
✓  you can see which account a programme, class or booking actually collects to — and which level decided it
✓  one consistent answer everywhere — payment email, QR code, widget, invoice and bank matching finally agree
✓  if you trade under more than one company: several profiles, each with its own bank accounts, and a different profile per class or booking
✓  groundwork for tracking income per entity — for the same case
WHICH ACCOUNT DOES A BOOKING COLLECT TO?

Default profile → Programme → Class → Booking

The lowest level that sets something wins; everything above it is inherited. Most companies set nothing at all and simply use the default — which is fine, and now visible.
Five minutes we recommend: open Settings → Billing → Invoice profiles and check three things — the legal details on each profile, the IBAN and account holder name on each bank account (banking apps verify the name when a client scans a payment QR code), and that the right account is marked as default.

If you issue invoices from Zooza, two things behave more consistently than before. A booking whose payments are managed by another booking now invoices under the paying booking's entity — previously these could differ. And if a product has its own invoice profile, that profile is now used for its invoices too, not only in the booking widget.

Nothing changes about payments you have already received. If something in your setup looks off, reply to this email or ping us in the app chat — we will look at it with you.
